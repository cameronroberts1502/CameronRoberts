Operator Dossier

The human behind the callsign.

I'm Cameron Roberts — a fourth-year Bachelor / Master of Electrical & Computer Engineering student at the University of Queensland. I live where firmware meets fabrication: copper traces, control loops, and the satisfying moment a board you laid out yourself boots for the first time.

My orbit is pulled toward space and space travel, rockets, defence, and motorsport — fields where the engineering has to be both audacious and unforgiving. That's the bar I hold my own work to: build hard things, make them reliable, and make them feel like the future.

Most of what you'll find on the flight deck I designed end-to-end — schematic capture, PCB layout, embedded firmware, and the interfaces that make the hardware legible to a human. I like the full stack of a physical product, from the first net to the last micro-interaction.
